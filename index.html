What Are Cookies?

Web cookies are small text files created by websites you visit. They store a variety of data, such as user preferences, session information, and tracking identifiers, on your browser.

How Cookies Work

When you visit a website, the server sends a Set-Cookie header in the HTTP response. Your browser stores this cookie and includes it in subsequent requests to the same server.

  1. Browser requests page from server.
  2. Server responds with content and Set-Cookie header.
  3. Browser stores cookie locally.
  4. Browser sends cookie with future requests to server.

Common Use Cases

Shopping Cart

Cookies remember the items you add to your cart as you browse the store.

User Sessions

Cookies maintain login sessions, so you stay signed in across pages.

Analytics & Tracking

Cookies help websites analyze user behavior to improve content and usability.

Consent and Regulations

Due to privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA, websites must inform users about cookie usage and obtain consent.
